# Corner Office

Business simulation — pick a business and grow it.

Made by **Pedro** with Claude.

- Play (Claude Artifact): https://claude.ai/artifact/8YkaWPmuNf17qM8PKSEVg2
- Source: `index.html` (single-file, open in any browser, works offline — no CDN requests)
- Fonts: `fonts/` (woff2, SIL OFL 1.1) are embedded into `index.html` by `python3 tools/embed-fonts.py`
